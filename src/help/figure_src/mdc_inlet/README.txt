Inlet help figures
==================

mdc_inlet_figs.py draws the figures of ../../MdcInlet.md into ../../images/: MdcInlet_types, _stations, _ramp, _hdiv, _diverter,
_phi.  It uses the helpers of ../mdc_bod/datcomfig.py, and the checks of what the page states are in
../mdc_bod/verify.py.  Nothing in this folder is installed; keep it free of .md files.

    cd src/help/figure_src/mdc_inlet
    <build>/venv/bin/python mdc_inlet_figs.py             # every figure, or name some (see FIGS at the end)

See ../mdc_bod/README.txt.
