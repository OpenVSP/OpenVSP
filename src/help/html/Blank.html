Blank

Contents

The Blank component has no shape. It is a coordinate frame -- an origin and a set of axes -- placed in the model like any other component, and drawn in the 3D view as an axis triad at its origin (red XX, green YY, blue ZZ), with one triad at each symmetric copy. With Pick on in the Geom Browser, click the triad to select the Blank.

What a Blank Is For

A Blank gives a model a frame that belongs to no part of the airframe. It is used to:

  • Hold a group of components together. Components attached to a Blank move with it, so an engine installation, a cockpit or a payload bay built under a Blank is moved or turned by changing the Blank alone. Put a Blank at the top of any assembly whose origin should not be on one of its parts.
  • Carry a mass that has no shape, such as equipment, fuel or passengers (see Point Mass).
  • Mark a point for components that refer to a point rather than a surface. A Routing point on a Blank lies at the Blank's origin, so a route can run through free space. An Auxiliary super cone or rotor fragment zone hung from a Blank sits at the Blank's origin with the Blank's axes, which is more flexible than the origin of the component it describes.

A Blank has no surface, so it is left out of every analysis that works on shape (CompGeom, DegenGeom, CFD meshing, export). Children attached to it by UW, RST, LMN or η\etaMN coordinates fall back to its origin and axes. A Blank cannot be flipped: its axes always stay right-handed.

Point Mass

A Blank's only mass is the concentrated mass of the Specified Mass Properties on the Mass tab (see Mass); the density, priority and thin-shell settings are greyed, since a Blank has no volume or area for them to act on. The center of gravity is measured from the Blank's origin in its own axes, so a Blank placed at an item of equipment carries that item's mass with its center of gravity at zero. While the Blank is selected and has a mass, the 3D view marks the center of gravity with a blue point.

Shared Tabs

The Gen, XForm and Mass tabs are common to every component; see Gen, XForm and Mass. A Blank has no Sub tab, since it has no surface to carry sub-surfaces.

Scripting

A script adds a Blank with AddGeom( "BLANK" ) and sets its point mass through the parms PointMass, CGx, CGy, CGz and Ixx to Iyz in the group Mass_Props.
